Change Password

This allows you to update your administrative access credentials. Your password is secured using industry-standard Argon2id hashing, bound to your hardware's unique signature and a secret system pepper.

  • Old Password: Required to verify your current identity.
  • New Password: Must be between 3 and 25 characters.

Note: Changing your password will instantly invalidate all other active sessions for security.

Background Music

Transform your captive portal into a live radio experience. This feature streams music to your customers' browsers with synchronized machine-specific timelines.

  • Radio Off: Disables all background audio.
  • Use Preset Radio: Streams default system tracks from the internal library.
  • Use Custom Files: Streams high-fidelity .mp3 files uploaded by the administrator.
Streaming Optimization

To avoid glitches, skips, or synchronization drifts, follow these technical standards:

  • Format: Strictly use MP3. Avoid M4A/AAC as they require full container parsing before seeking.
  • Bitrate: Recommended 128k to 192k. Higher bitrates may cause buffering on weak client signals, while lower bitrates may sound distorted.

Recommended Download Command: Use yt-dlp for professional-grade conversion and metadata embedding:

yt-dlp -vU -f bestaudio --extract-audio --audio-format mp3 --audio-quality 192K --embed-metadata --ffmpeg-location /usr/bin YOUTUBE_URL

Admin Restriction

A second layer of security for your dashboard. This restricts who can even see the login screen based on their hardware MAC address.

  • Open Access: Any device on the network can attempt to log in.
  • Restricted: Only devices in your Whitelist can access the /admin directory. All others will be redirected to an "Access Denied" barrier.

Pro Tip: Always add your current device to the whitelist BEFORE enabling Restricted mode to prevent accidental lockout.

Critical Warning: Admin Compromise

Unauthorized access to the dashboard is the primary vector for system-wide compromise. If an attacker gains entry, they can dismantle your entire security perimeter:

  • Security Bypass: An attacker can disable Admin Restrictions and SSH Gates, leaving the system permanently open to remote scanners and botnets.
  • Logic Poisoning: Through the dashboard, logic modules can be manipulated to create permanent "FreeNet" backdoors for specific devices, bypassing the vending algorithm entirely.
  • Credential Exposure: Access to the settings allows for the modification of authentication parameters, potentially leading to a persistent loss of control over the hardware.

The Importance of Admin Restriction: MAC-based whitelisting is your strongest defense. It ensures that even if your password is stolen, the dashboard remains invisible and inaccessible to unauthorized hardware. This "Dual-Gate" approach is essential for a production distro.
